## Roles (rotate left each chapter)
- **Narrator** (= Active Player): frames scene, describes surroundings, plays supporting chars, reveals the Clue.
- **Witness** (left of Narrator): plays the main character, follows Clues, speaks Fears & Rationalizations after each scene.
- **Whisperers** (everyone else): add atmosphere, answer Narrator's questions, play supporting chars, flag risk. Don't invent Clues.

## Setup
1. Ban Lists (Hard / Soft). ERASE + STOP cards always in play.
2. Pick scenario OR build from Core Decks: 3 Locations + 3 Characters + main character sheet (name, description, reason here, personality, source of strength).
3. Build Mystery Deck (15 cards). Deal hands: **3p → 3 cards each; 4–5p → 2 each**. Everyone must have ≥1 Clue card.
4. Lay out Story Track. Flip Story Card 1, follow card prompts throughout.

## Structure (3 parts)
1. **Signs & Portents** — 5 Investigation Chapters.
2. **Impending Doom** — 1–3 Investigation Chapters, ends when Endgame Rule triggered.
3. **The Horror Revealed** — Journey Into Darkness → Final Horror → Epilogue → Share Conclusions.

## Investigation Chapter loop
a. Play the scene. **Narrator must reveal one new Clue** (play a Clue Card, OR from scene 2+ expand an existing Clue).  
b. **Fears & Rationalizations** — Witness voices the character's inner monologue.  
c. **Leap to Conclusions** — everyone secretly writes/updates their theory: what, who, doing what, why.  
d. Pass Active Player Token left; advance Story Token.

## Active rules by phase

| Phase | Rules in play |
|---|---|
| Always | Roles, Isolation (no card-sharing, no meta-discussion), Risk, ERASE, STOP |
| From Investigation 1 | + Clue Rule |
| Signs & Portents | + **Creeping Horror** (nothing supernatural directly witnessed; always deniable; MC can't admit supernatural) + **No Deliberate Harm** (no harm to humans; MC can't die) |
| Impending Doom | Drop Creeping Horror + No Deliberate Harm. Add **Survival & Sanity** (supernatural may be directly seen but unclear/ambiguous/doubtful; harm allowed but MC can't die) + **Endgame Rule** |

## Risk Rule (anyone, anytime in a scene)
Trigger → describe a risk → Witness states hope → Witness + Whisperers each suggest one of: *fall short / pay a price / complication* → Narrator picks one or more.

## Endgame trigger
Narrator (unwillingly) or Witness (by choice) moves MC into a dark/isolated/ancient/creepy place. Mandatory by end of 3rd Impending Doom scene.

## Journey Into Darkness
- Flip Role Cards face-down — no roles.
- Go around table; each player adds a few sentences describing either: **the environment deepening** or **MC's fears & rationalizations**.
- No new Clues.
- From step 13 the Active Player may end it (MC arrives at the horror location); must end by step 16.

## Final Horror
- Drop Survival & Sanity. Anything goes, including MC death.
- Active Player must have an idea — a scene that **reveals the monster/conspiracy** and/or **a worldview-shattering truth**, drawn from their Conclusions. If they don't, pass token until someone does.
- Plays like an Investigation scene (no new Clues). Any attempt to defeat the horror auto-fails (unless *Defeat the Evil* Special Card played; Risk Rule still applies).

## Epilogue
Narrated, not played. Witness: MC's aftermath (or legacy if dead). Narrator: how the horror persists/spreads. Whisperers add atmosphere.

## Share Conclusions
Everyone reveals their theories. Optionally replay the Final Horror with a different Narrator's Conclusions.

## Special Cards
Break specific rules as written on the card; played during the specified chapter. May or may not double as a Clue, but only **one Clue per scene** either way.

## Timing (standard)
Setup 15–30m · Investigation chapters 10–15m each · Journey 10–15m · Final Horror 10–15m · Epilogue 5–10m · Share 5–10m.  
Speedrun: 3 S&P + 2 ID chapters ≈ 90m.
